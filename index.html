Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

GuardMyMoney helps users organize subscription information, review recurring charges, and set reminders. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the GuardMyMoney mobile application (the “App”).

1. Information you enter and import

You may enter subscription names, prices, billing dates, trial details, and reminder preferences, or import screenshots to help identify subscription information. The App is designed to analyze imported screenshots on your device using locally bundled text-recognition tools. Saved subscription details are stored in the App’s local device storage. Imported images are used to extract information for your review; they are not intended to be uploaded to GuardMyMoney servers for recognition.

Subscription records may include information you choose to enter or confirm, such as provider names, billing amounts, and renewal dates. These records are used to display your subscriptions, summarize spending, and calculate reminders.

2. Purchases and subscription access

Premium subscriptions are purchased through Apple’s in-app purchase system. GuardMyMoney uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service, to process purchase status, validate entitlement, restore purchases, and support subscription analytics. Apple and RevenueCat may process transaction records, receipts, subscription status, an app-specific customer identifier, and related technical information needed to deliver these services. GuardMyMoney does not receive your full payment-card number from Apple in-app purchases.

RevenueCat’s privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy. Apple’s privacy information is available at apple.com/legal/privacy.

3. Reminders and notifications

If you allow notifications, the App uses your saved dates and reminder preferences to schedule subscription reminders on your device. You can manage notification permissions in iOS Settings. Notifications are informational; GuardMyMoney cannot cancel or modify subscriptions held with other providers.

4. How information is used

  • To import, organize, display, and calculate information about subscriptions you choose to track.
  • To schedule reminders you request.
  • To verify Premium access, restore purchases, and prevent unauthorized access to paid features.
  • To review aggregated subscription and purchase analytics supplied by the payment-management provider.
  • To respond if you contact us for assistance.

5. Advertising and tracking

GuardMyMoney is not designed to use your imported screenshots or locally saved subscription entries for targeted advertising. Purchase information processed through RevenueCat is used for app functionality and subscription analytics, not cross-app advertising tracking by GuardMyMoney. If the App's services or data practices materially change, this policy will be updated and applicable disclosures will be revised.

6. Sharing and service providers

Payment-related information is processed by Apple and RevenueCat to provide in-app subscriptions. GuardMyMoney does not sell your locally saved subscription records or imported screenshots to advertisers. Service providers process information under their own applicable policies and contractual obligations.

7. Retention and deletion

Locally saved subscription information remains on your device until removed through the App or by removing the App and its stored data, subject to any iOS backups or device-restoration settings. Purchase records may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat in accordance with their policies and applicable legal obligations. For questions or requests about information we control, contact us using the address below.

8. Security

We seek to limit handling of subscription records to what is needed for App functionality. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your device and Apple account protected and review screenshots before importing them.

9. Children

GuardMyMoney is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its integrations, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
